How Grading Works

Grades and grade points are auto-computed from the score you enter. A student's CGPA is recalculated each time you view their transcript, using the weighted formula: Σ(GP × CU) / Σ(CU).

■ 3.50 – 4.49 · Second Class Upper
■ 2.50 – 3.49 · Second Class Lower
■ 1.50 – 2.49 · Third Class
